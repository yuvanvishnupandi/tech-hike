FAQs

Questions? Answers.

Your most frequently asked questions, all in one place. If you don't see what you need, reach out to us.

What does it cost, and how long is the contract? + −
Tech Hike runs on a flat 4-year institutional license — one price, locked for four years, with no per-seat or per-year renewal like LeetCode or HackerRank charge. That means the cost per student drops every year as your batch strength grows, and your Principal never has to budget for a surprise renewal hike.
How much work is onboarding? Do we need weeks of setup? + −
Onboarding is instant. We sync with your existing student database, and your campus is live within 48 hours. No complex IT integrations needed.
Is our students’ data safe, and who can see what? + −
Yes, we are fully GDPR and SOC-2 compliant. Only authorized HODs and Admins from your institution can access your student data and performance reports.
Where do the questions come from, and are they kept current? + −
Our questions are sourced directly from actual placement drives by our industry partners. We update our question banks quarterly to ensure they perfectly match current industry trends.
How do you stop students from cheating during a test? + −
We use enterprise-grade proctoring including tab-switch detection, copy-paste blocking, and full focus tracking. Every test generates a detailed proctor log for the instructor.
What support and training does our staff get? + −
Every institution gets a dedicated Account Manager. We provide initial faculty training sessions and 24/7 technical support for any issues that arise during exams.
How is this different from LeetCode or SkillRack? + −
While others focus purely on individual coding practice, Tech Hike is built specifically for college placements. We offer full HOD controls, campus-wide leaderboards, and exact exam mock-ups for TCS, Infosys, and more.
Do students need to install anything on their laptop? + −
Absolutely nothing. Everything, including the multi-language Monaco code editor, runs entirely in the browser. They just log in and start coding.
